# Offshore Wind Development

Developer-side learning notes for bottom-fixed offshore wind, with a focus on wind turbine generator (WTG) selection, foundation engineering, and their design interfaces.

## Scope and reading order

1. [Development overview and WBS](OffshoreWindDevelopment_note.md): project stages, illustrative gates, and a deliberately high-level work breakdown structure.
2. [WTG engineering](WTG.md): OEM platform development, project-specific selection, site suitability, and turbine–foundation interfaces.
3. [Foundation engineering](FOU.md): concept selection, integrated loads, design verification, and release criteria.
4. [WTG–foundation worked example](WTG_Foundation_example.md): an illustrative decision and load-iteration workflow.
5. Supporting OSS notes: [WBS overview](OSS/OSS.md) and [concept tutorial](OSS/Tutorial%20concept%20design.md). These remain secondary to the WTG/foundation focus.

## How to use these notes

These are educational notes, not an approved design basis or an exhaustive engineering checklist. The lifecycle coverage runs from origination through operations handover. Detailed operations, life extension, and decommissioning are outside the present scope.

Gate names, responsibilities, schedules, and acceptance criteria are examples to adapt to the project's contracts, jurisdiction, and certification scheme. Numerical examples are illustrative; they do not establish equipment suitability. Technical references are recorded in the [reference register](REFERENCES.md); agree the applicable editions before using them on a project.

The WBS remains at its existing overview depth. Engineering detail belongs in the WTG and foundation guides rather than additional WBS branches.

| AEP / LCOE | Annual energy production / levelized cost of energy |
