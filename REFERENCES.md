# Technical reference register

[Home](README.md)

This register identifies sources and their intended use. Public descriptions were checked on 27 October 2026; this is not a clause-by-clause verification of the notes. A project must record its agreed editions, amendments, local requirements, and certification scope in its own design basis.

| Reference | Edition / status recorded here | Use in these notes |
|---|---|---|
| [IEC 61400-3-1:2019](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/29360) | 2019; project to confirm applicable amendments and edition | Fixed offshore wind turbine design requirements; starting point for the offshore design basis and load-case matrix. |
| IEC 61400-1 | Project to select edition and amendments | General turbine design requirements; use with the applicable offshore requirements. |
| [DNV-ST-0126](https://www.dnv.com/energy/standards-guidelines/dnv-st-0126-support-structures-for-wind-turbines) | Public listing: 2021-12; project to confirm | Wind turbine support structure design and verification framework. |
| [DNV wind farm project certification](https://www.dnv.com/services/renewables-certification/wind-farm-project-certification/) | Service overview; scheme and edition to be agreed | Distinguishes site-specific environmental, integrated-system, manufacturing, installation, and commissioning assessments. |
| [TÜV NORD type certification overview](https://www.tuev-nord.de/en/dienstleistungen/auditierung-und-zertifizierung/windenergie/zertifizierung/type-certification/) | Service overview; scheme and edition to be agreed | Background on turbine type certification modules. |

For each project, additionally identify the governing geotechnical, fatigue, corrosion, electrical, grid, marine operation, and local regulatory requirements. Resolve conflicts through an agreed hierarchy of requirements rather than combining individual clauses without review.
